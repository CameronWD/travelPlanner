One hue ramp for categories and chapters

Don't collapse the two lists, and don't make two ramps. There's one 8-hue ramp plus stone. The four brand accents are in it at their exact values. Categories map onto it with a fixed mapping. Chapters let people pick any of the 8 hues. Stored values don't change: the legacy Tailwind names map 1:1 (amber → sun, emerald → leaf, violet → lilac, rose → pink, orange → coral). Fills always get ink text and a 2px ink outline. Use -text when the hue sits on paper as text or an icon. Colour is never the only signal: categories carry an icon and chapters carry their name.

B3

Map palette

lib/map-palette.ts · lib/map-pins.ts

Hex is allowed only in map-palette.ts, and every value there is commented with the token it mirrors. Keep the tiles on CARTO light/dark. Pins are stickers: 2px ink outline, 2px hard shadow and a glyph, never a colour on its own. Routes are an ink casing (7px) with a chapter-hue line (3.5px) on top. pinHex(category, dark?) keeps its old signature.

B7

Outlined wordmark

public/brand/*.svg · components/ui/logo-paths.ts · logo.tsx

The wordmark is Bricolage Grotesque 800 at −0.04em tracking, now outlined to paths. logo.tsx, the OG card and global-error all read the same path constants, so nothing depends on the web font. The word takes currentColor and the dot is always coral. safari-pinned-tab.svg stays as the mark only: it's a square mono mask, and a wordmark would be unreadable at 16px.
