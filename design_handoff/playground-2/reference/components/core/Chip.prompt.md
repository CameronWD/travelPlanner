Sticker chip — pill with a 2px outline; the workhorse for statuses, tags, transport legs and filters.

```jsx
<Chip tone="lilac">Zz Nohga Ueno ✓</Chip>
<Chip tone="sun">→ Romancecar · 09:00</Chip>
<Chip uppercase tone="teal" size="s">Kanto</Chip>
<Chip dashed>+ chapter</Chip>
```

Colour follows meaning: lilac = stays (Zz), sun = transport/to-dos/money, teal = chapters/done, coral = something missing. Chips are stickers: they may overlap a card edge (position absolute, top −10px).
