# Shared view: one route, Home at both ends, Stops that open independently, 2026-11-25

Agreed with Cam on 25 Nov after he looked at the shared page with its new travel cards.

## What changes

1. **Home at both ends.** When the link draws a leg from the Home base, a dashed Home card sits above it; when it draws a leg back, one sits below. The card shows a house icon, the Home base's name and the word "Home", like the Plan's Home base bookend. The connector line starts and ends at these cards. No card without its leg; no Home name, no card (the leg then stands alone as today). Nothing else about Home is shown: no address, no timezone.
2. **Stops open independently.** Opening a Stop no longer closes another. The Stop that starts open is unchanged (the current Stop during the trip, the first before it, none after). The Stop picker and index still jump to a Stop and open it, leaving the others as they are.
3. **One list.** "The route" and "Day by day" become one section headed "The route": the Stop cards and travel cards, each Stop opening to show its days.
   - A Stop card shows its country: on a closed card it is the last part of the summary line ("Platzl Hotel · 7 plans · Germany"), and on an open card it follows the name line as today's sub-line does.
   - Stops with no dates yet, which only the old route list showed, come at the end of the list as plain, non-opening cards with their name, country, nights when known, and "Dates to come".
   - **Exception:** a link with accommodation, transport and daily plans all off has nothing to open. It keeps today's simple route list, unchanged.
4. The page reads: header, Right now (during the trip), map, the route, the footer.

## What does not change

Every privacy rule on the page; the link's switches; the Right now card; the map; the travel cards; what an open Stop shows.
