# Refunds on a Cost, 2026-11-25

Agreed with Cam on 25 Nov. Deleting a Transport, Accommodation or Item whose Cost has been paid turns that Cost into an Other cost named after the deleted thing, without saying so, and the app has no way to record that money came back. Cam asked for a refund choice on delete; the interview widened it: a refund is something that happens to a payment, so it belongs to the Cost.

## 1. What a Refund is

- A **paid** Cost can carry one **Refund**: an amount in the Cost's own currency, and a state.
- **Expected**: promised, not yet arrived. It changes no total.
- **Received**: arrived, on a date the Traveller gives. Only a received Refund counts.
- A Refund is at least 1 minor unit and never more than the paid amount.
- A Cost with a Refund cannot be marked unpaid, and its paid amount cannot be lowered below the Refund, until the Refund is removed.
- The currency of a Cost with a Refund cannot be changed until the Refund is removed.

Stored on `Cost` as `refundMinor Int?` and `refundedAt DateTime?`: a `refundMinor` with no `refundedAt` is expected; with one, received.

## 2. Totals use the net

With `received` = the Refund amount when it is received and the Cost is paid, else 0:

- the Cost's cost amount counts as `max(0, costMinor − received)`;
- its paid amount counts as `max(0, paidMinor − received)`.

Every total reads those: Trip cost, Spend so far, the Budget's roll-ups by category, Stop, Chapter and day, the settlement split, the Home tiles, Summary, the printed plan, and what Claude's tools report. A Refund converts to the Home currency at its Cost's own snapshotted rate (ADR 0002). On the by-day view a Refund reduces the Cost on the Cost's own day.

## 3. How a refunded Cost is shown

A Cost still shows one current number (CONTEXT.md **Cost**): its net. Beside it, a tag: "Refunded A$50", or "A$50 refund expected". Opening the Cost shows the paid amount, the Refund and its date.

## 4. Recording a Refund

- From any paid Cost: on the Money page's rows and in the Cost dialogs for an owned Cost and an Other cost.
- A dialog titled "Refund": "Refund amount" (pre-filled with the paid amount), "Expected" or "Received", "Date received" (today) when received; Save; "Remove refund" when one exists.
- An expected Refund offers "Received", which asks for the date.
- Messages: "A refund can't be more than what was paid." / "Mark it paid before recording a refund." / "Remove the refund before marking this unpaid."

## 5. Deleting something that was paid for

For a Transport, an Accommodation or an Item with at least one ever-paid Cost, the delete confirmation asks: "You've paid {amount} for this. What happened to the money?"

| Answer | Hint shown | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Not refunded (default) | It stays in your spend as an Other cost. | As today: Other cost, labelled "{label} (deleted)" |
| Refund expected | It stays in your spend until the refund arrives. | Other cost "{label} (cancelled)" with an expected Refund |
| Refunded | It comes off your spend. | Other cost "{label} (cancelled)" with a received Refund, dated |

"Refund amount" is pre-filled with everything paid; with several paid Costs each has its own amount, and an empty amount means nothing came back on that Cost. A Cost that already holds a Refund is not asked about and keeps it. A converted Cost counts only what was paid: its cost amount becomes its paid amount. A thing with no ever-paid Cost keeps today's confirmation. Unpaid Costs are still deleted with their owner (ADR 0039).

Deleting a whole Stop does not ask: it behaves as today, and a Refund is recorded afterwards on the Other cost it leaves.

## 6. Money page

- "Refunds you're waiting on": the expected Refunds, each with its amount, what it is for and "Received". Shown only when there is at least one.
- The "Refunded" group, in the To pay card and the All costs sheet, holds any Cost with a received Refund whose shown amount is zero.

## 7. Elsewhere

- Claude's tools: `record_refund`, `mark_refund_received`, `remove_refund`; `list_costs` and the owner reads show a Cost's Refund and net; `delete_transport`, `delete_accommodation` and `delete_thing_to_do` accept the same answer as section 5.
- Activity records a Refund being recorded, received and removed.
- A Fork copies no Refund, as it copies no payment.

## 8. Not in this work

- More than one Refund per Cost.
- A Home-currency amount for a Refund that differs from the Cost's rate.
- Asking per Accommodation when a Stop is deleted.
- Reminders or Digest lines for an overdue expected Refund.
- Refund fields on the inline cost row inside the Transport, Accommodation and Item forms: those forms keep a Cost's Refund untouched and refuse a change the rules in section 1 forbid.

## 9. Deploy

Two nullable columns on `Cost`: additive, no backfill. Same order as §4n in DEPLOY.md: the migration commit to `main` first, then the branch to beta, then `main`.

## 10. How it is checked

Unit tests per piece, with the Budget's sum invariant extended to refunded Costs; the branch tier once; a browser pass on the local demo trip (record, receive, remove, delete with each answer, light and dark); then Cam cancels and refunds something real on beta before it reaches `main`.
