- **Claude connection · Japan Autumn Dream 🍁 Temples & Tastes · Cam · 2026-10-15** — `cmva5yfw4000007lawnx4y88x`
  Feature idea: an AI "Review trip" feature, both as an in-app button and as an MCP endpoint (e.g. review_trip(tripId)), that audits a whole trip the way Claude just did manually for the Japan Autumn Dream trip.
  
  Today get_flags catches structural issues (backtracks, spread-out days, missing accommodation), but a full review needed many manual steps: reading the whole plan, web searches, and fixing things by hand. What that review caught that flags didn't:
  - Day-of-week closures: a market whose shops mostly close on Wednesdays was scheduled on a Wednesday. Same idea for museums closed on Mondays, restaurants closed on Sundays, etc.
  - Seasonal/event windows: a night illumination scheduled after its season ended, and another on what may be its last night.
  - Pass/ticket coverage gaps: a 2-day transit pass across a 3-calendar-day stay, so the final leg wasn't covered.
  - Seasonal opening hours (e.g. a ropeway with shorter winter hours than its summer timetable).
  - Public holidays in the destination and home country during the trip, plus how many leave days are needed.
  - Rule/policy changes affecting the trip (e.g. a country's tax-free shopping switching to an airport refund, so more airport time is needed).
  - Stale notes left over after the itinerary was restructured (notes still referencing an old route/stop order).
  - Logistics sanity: luggage forwarding timing, check-in vs. arrival times, airport buffer before flights.
  - Missing packing/pre-trip items based on destination weather (e.g. umbrella for a rainy city).
  
  Suggested shape:
  - MCP: review_trip(tripId, scope?) returning structured findings: { severity, category (closure | season | ticket | holiday | logistics | stale_note | packing | budget), itemId/stopId/transportId/date, title, detail, suggestedFix, sourceUrl }, so an assistant can apply fixes and cite sources.
  - In-app: a "Review my trip" button that runs the same check (Claude API + web search) and shows findings as a checklist with one-tap "apply fix" / "dismiss", re-runnable before departure (e.g. auto-suggest ~1 month out when event dates are published).
  - Could extend get_flags with a 'deep' mode rather than a new endpoint.
