the Day carousel gets ten days on real phones before the trip leaves on 4 Dec.

Nothing from the sweep is in this spec.

## 1. Day carousel is one continuous, loaded carousel

Note `cmwmyah6t000008ifgca9vw8m`. Amends ADR 0065.

Today the Day view renders three days and navigates on every swipe; each settle is a
cold server render, the header and Day strip lag, and a second swipe stalls.

- First paint is unchanged: the shown day and its two neighbours, rendered by the server.
- The scroller has one slot per day of the trip from first paint. Slots are fixed
  width, so filling one never moves the scroll position.
- Remaining days fill in the background, nearest first: the next ring of two, then the
  rest. An unfilled slot shows a skeleton.
- A settled swipe changes the header, Day strip and URL in the browser, with no server
  navigation (`window.history.pushState`, which this Next version integrates with
  `usePathname`). Back and forward step through days. A fling may cross several days.
- The animated weather scene and the Day map run only for the shown day and its
  neighbours.
- Any edit refreshes the loaded days rather than trusting them.
- Offline or on a slow network, fills come from the saved copy where one exists (ADR
  0077); a day that cannot be filled falls back to navigating, as today.
- Desktop keeps its current shape (one day, Day strip as the carousel) and gains the
  same loaded days and URL behaviour.
- A Surprise day stays as hidden in a filled slot as it is on its own page.

Done when: on a phone, after the fills land, swiping through five consecutive days
makes no document or RSC navigation request and the header and strip match the day on
screen at every settle.

## 2. "More to book" opens the whole list

Note `cmwmyjpfi000107l3et894iuh`.

The Still to book card's "more" link opens a sheet on trip home (bottom sheet on
phones, dialog on desktop) instead of going to Plan.

- Everything still To book on the trip: Transport, Accommodation and things to do.
- Order: overdue Book by first, then by Book by date, then undated.
- Each row: what it is, its Stop or day, its Book by date. Tapping opens that thing on
  Plan through the deep link Plan already understands.
- Surprise contents stay hidden from the surprised Traveller.

## 3. Reminders are dismissed with "Got it"

Note `cmwmyi83s000408ifwc03xcc1`. CONTEXT.md **Reminder** is already updated.

- Migration: one nullable timestamp on `Reminder` for when it was dismissed.
- Trip home: each Reminder shows **Got it** and Edit. Got it dismisses for both
  Travellers.
- A dismissed Reminder leaves trip home and is not read out in the Digest.
- It stays on the thing it is about in Plan, shown as dismissed, with a way to bring
  it back.
- Delete moves inside Edit and asks first, as the Plan dialogs already do.
- Trip home keeps showing every upcoming Reminder (the glossary now says so).
- Dismiss and bring-back are recorded as Activity like other Reminder changes, and
  are available through the Claude connection wherever Reminders are edited there.

Because of the migration: deploy main, confirm, then push beta.

## 4. Feedback panel fills the space above the keyboard

Note `cmwmy9ev0000007l80p2mduf0`.

On a phone with the keyboard up, the Feedback panel grows to fill the visible area
above the keyboard; no band of blurred backdrop above a short strip. Keyboard down, it
returns to content height. The stale comments in the launcher are corrected. Other
phone sheets on the same primitive are left alone here; the sweep reviews them.

Cannot be proven in Playwright. Cam checks it on a real iPhone.

## 5. Weather motion keeps to one side

Note `cmwmybjp6000108ifl4va7vvb`.

text, for the card's full height, on both the compact and regular card. No moving
shape passes under or beside any line of text.

Note `cmwmycnxo000007l327uvbwg7`.

## 8. Loading states

Note `cmwmyhjrb000308ifdcft01i7`. Amends ADR 0063; the one-`loading.tsx` rule stands.

Trips list:
- The install nudge, the map card's phone height, the carousel dots and the tally
  strip all have their space reserved before hydration.
- The phone skeleton matches the page: same card height, dots, tally strip.

Every page inside a trip, opened cold:
- The single loading file shows the Trips list skeleton for `/trips` and, for any path
  inside a trip, the Teepee logo spinning in the centre of the shell. The page then
  appears at once. The logo is still under reduced motion.
- Moving between sections and days is unchanged: the current page holds.

Done when: cumulative layout shift on `/trips` at 402px is under 0.05 on a normal
load, and a cold open of trip home shows the logo and never the Trips list skeleton.
