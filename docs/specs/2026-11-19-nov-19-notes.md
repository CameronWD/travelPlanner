## 1. Day carousel is one continuous, loaded carousel

Note `cmwmyah6t000008ifgca9vw8m`. Amends ADR 0065 (amendment 2026-11-19).

Before: the Day view rendered three days and every settle was a cold server render that
remounted the page; the header and Day strip lagged, and a second swipe stalled.

- First paint is unchanged: the shown day and its two neighbours, rendered by the server.
- The scroller has one fixed-width slot per day of the trip from first paint, so filling
  one never moves the scroll position. Seven days are filled around the day shown: the
  days two and three away stream in behind it, every other slot is a skeleton. Warm copies
  saved for offline stay three-day.
- The address stays `/trips/<ref>/day/<date>`; a rewrite hands the date to one Day page,
  so a day change remounts nothing.
- A settled swipe, arrow, key or chip shows that day's header (a date-only one until its
  slot fills) and lights the Day strip at once, then navigates there straight away with an
  ordinary router push, without the progress bar. A fling may cross several days. When the
  navigation lands the window re-centres: slots entering it fill, slots leaving it become
  skeletons, and nothing the Traveller is touching moves. Back and forward step through
  days from the router cache.
- No `window.history.pushState`: a URL the router did not navigate to breaks refresh and
  Back.
- Days more than one slot from the day in view pause their animations and are inert; the
  Day map mounts only for the day the page shows.
- Any edit refreshes the page in place: the day, its scroll position and the address hold.
- A navigation that fails or never ends parks the carousel back on the day shown, as before.
- Desktop keeps its shape (one day, Day strip as the carousel): a day change shows the new
  day and its header at once with a crossfade, then makes the same push.
- A Surprise day stays as hidden in a filled slot as it is on its own page.

Done when: on a phone, swiping through five consecutive days keeps the carousel mounted,
the header and strip match the day on screen at every settle before the server answers,
and a navigation landing under a new swipe does not interrupt it.

## 2. "More to book" opens the whole list

Note `cmwmyjpfi000107l3et894iuh`.

The Still to book card's "more" link opens a sheet on trip home (bottom sheet on
phones, dialog on desktop) instead of going to Plan.

- Everything still To book on the trip: Transport, Accommodation and things to do.
- Order: overdue Book by first, then by Book by date, then undated.
- Each row is the row the card already draws (what it is, Book by, when it happens)
  and goes where the card's rows already go: a dated thing to its day, an undated one
  to its Stop on Plan.
- Surprise contents stay hidden from the surprised Traveller.

## 3. Reminders are dismissed with "Got it"

Note `cmwmyi83s000408ifwc03xcc1`. CONTEXT.md **Reminder** is already updated.

- Migration: one nullable timestamp on `Reminder` for when it was dismissed.
- Trip home: each Reminder shows **Got it** and Edit. Got it dismisses for both
  Travellers.
- A dismissed Reminder leaves trip home and is not read out in the Digest.
- It stays on the thing it is about in Plan, shown as dismissed, with a way to bring
  it back.
- Delete moves inside Edit and asks first, as the Plan dialogs already do.
- Trip home keeps showing every upcoming Reminder (the glossary now says so).
- Dismiss and bring-back are recorded as Activity like other Reminder changes, and
  are available through the Claude connection wherever Reminders are edited there.

Because of the migration: deploy main, confirm, then push beta.

## 8. Loading states

Note `cmwmyhjrb000308ifdcft01i7`. Amends ADR 0063; the one-`loading.tsx` rule stands.

Trips list:
- The install nudge, the map card's phone height, the carousel dots and the tally
  strip all have their space reserved before hydration.
- The phone skeleton matches the page: same card height, dots, tally strip.

Every page inside a trip, opened cold:
- The single loading file shows the Trips list skeleton for `/trips` and, for any path
  inside a trip, the Teepee logo spinning in the centre of the shell. The page then
  appears at once. The logo is still under reduced motion.
- Moving between sections and days is unchanged: the current page holds.

Done when: cumulative layout shift on `/trips` at 402px is under 0.05 on a normal
load, and a cold open of trip home shows the logo and never the Trips list skeleton.
