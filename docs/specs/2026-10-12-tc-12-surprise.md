## What a Surprise is (CONTEXT.md, in one breath)

A Stop or an Item that one Traveller (the **planner**) is arranging and that
a chosen set of other Travellers (the **surprised**, default: everyone else)
may see the outside of but not the inside. Revealed by the planner at any
moment, or by itself the day after it is over, or by deletion, or by the
planner leaving. Once revealed it is ordinary everywhere at once.

Decisions, in the order they were made:

1. Surprised Travellers are a **set**, default everyone but the planner; a
   later joiner is not surprised unless added; the planner is never in it.
2. Grain is **Stop or Item**. Not day, not Accommodation, not Transport.
3. The surprised see a **read-only shell**: Stop name, dates, nights, legs
   in and out; Item date and time. They cannot edit, delete, pin, re-date or
   add inside. Ripple still moves the Stop. Non-surprised Travellers work
   inside as normal and whatever they add is inside. **Only the planner**
   reveals or changes the set; the owner gets no peek.
4. **Calendar feed** (one per Trip, bearer token): hides the inside for
   everyone until reveal.
5. **Budget**: hidden Costs count in the grand total, by-Stop and by-day
   rows; the by-Category breakdown gathers them into one row *Surprise*.
   Lines, upcoming payments, Digest payment reminders and `list_costs` omit
   them for the surprised.
6. **Share links** show every audience the shell only, until reveal.
7. **Reveal**: by the planner any time; automatically the day *after* the
   Stop's depart date / the Item's date, in the Stop's timezone (never on
   the day); rough Stops and undated things to do only by hand; sticky.
   Delete reveals; the planner leaving or being removed reveals. Hidden
   Activity becomes visible in place; one new line "revealed the surprise in
   Paris"; the old lines do not count as unread. The planner's Home nudges
   "Reveal the surprise in Paris?" on the arrive date / Item's day.
8. **Forks**: the state rides with the Stop/Item through fork, promote and
   Duplicate; each copy is its own Surprise. Nothing more for Forks.
9. **Claude connection**: reads filtered like the app; the plan marks the
   shell "Surprise, planned by Cam" (surprised view) or "Surprise for Sam"
   (planner view); instructions tell Claude not to suggest, research or ask
   about a Surprise it is surprised by; writes inside are refused by the
   same guard. Two new tools: `set_surprise`, `reveal_surprise`.
10. **UI**: Stop actions menu entry "Make it a surprise" / "Reveal the
    surprise"; Item form control "Surprise for…" beside Hidden from shares;
    who-picker only when the Trip has more than two Travellers; badges;
    the surprised see the shell with one line and a gift icon.

## Part 1 · Model

A. `Stop` and `Item` gain three columns, same names on both:
   - `surprisePlannerId String?` — the planner's User id. Non-null means
     "this is (or was) a Surprise".
   - `surpriseForIds String?` — JSON array of User ids (schema convention:
     JSON as String). Never contains the planner.
   - `surpriseRevealedAt DateTime?` — set by a hand reveal, the cron, a
     delete, or the planner leaving. Null while live.
   Additive migration; nothing dropped.
B. `Activity` gains `surpriseOfType String?` ("STOP" | "ITEM") and
   `surpriseOfId String?`: the Surprise this row is *inside*, stamped at
   write time by `recordPlanActivity` (resolve the entity to its owning
   Stop/Item; an Item that is itself a Surprise stamps itself; a Stop's own
   row stamps the Stop). Rows whose Surprise is live and whose viewer is
   surprised are hidden; after reveal the same rows pass. The row written
   *for* the reveal has no stamp. `entityLabel` snapshots already survive
   deletion so nothing else changes.
C. No Prisma `enum`, no `Json` — consistent with the schema note in
   `docs/HANDOFF.md`.

## Part 2 · One visibility helper, used by every reader

A. `lib/surprise.ts`, pure, unit-tested:
   - `surpriseState(row, now)` → `"none" | "live" | "revealed"`, where
     "live" also requires `now` (in the Stop's timezone, falling back to the
     Trip's home timezone, then UTC) to be on or before the auto-reveal day
     (Stop: depart date; Item: its date; undated: live until hand reveal).
   - `isSurprisedBy(row, viewerId)` — viewerId in `surpriseForIds` and
     state is live. `viewerId === null` (a Share link, the Calendar feed)
     counts as surprised by every live Surprise.
   - `hiddenFor(viewerId, stops, items, now)` → `{ stopIds, itemIds }`:
     the Stops and Items whose inside the viewer may not see (Items inside a
     hidden Stop are hidden too, as are Surprise Items of their own).
   - Prisma `where` fragments built from that set: `notInsideSurprise(ids)`
     for Item/Accommodation/Reminder/DayTitle by `stopId`, and for Note and
     Attachment by `(targetType, targetId)` against the hidden entity ids.
   - `shellOf(stop)` / `shellOf(item)` — the allowed fields only, plus
     `surprise: { plannerId, forIds, revealedAt }` so the UI can badge.
B. Every reader filters **in the query** (ADR 0051's rule), computing the
   hidden set from the Stops and Items it already loads, then applying the
   fragments to its remaining queries. Readers (from the survey):
   - Pages: plan, day/[date] (`day-view-loader`), calendar, summary, print,
     budget (`budget-loader`), wishlist (the "in this plan" marker too),
     files (`files-index-loader`), activity, checklists (Reminders),
     compare (`getComparison`), Home loaders (desktop, travelling, past),
     `next-steps-loader`, `flags-loader`, `nav-counts`, `home-map-stops`,
     `trip-shell-reads` (bell), `journal-loader` (untouched: Journal is not
     inside a Stop), the layout's offline warm list.
   - `server/actions/search.ts`, `server/actions/ai.ts`.
   - `app/api/attachments/[id]/route.ts`: a file attached inside a live
     Surprise is 404 to the surprised.
   - Share page and share journal-photo route (viewerId null).
   - Calendar feed `buildICS` input (viewerId null).
   - Digest: `collectDigestInput` gains `userId`; `dispatchDigest` passes it.
   - MCP reads: `trip-plan.ts`, `costs.ts`, `attachments.ts`,
     `attachment-content.ts`, `get_notes`, `get_activity`, `get_wishlist`,
     and the reused loaders inherit it.
C. A drift guard test, in the style of the existing guards: every module in
   a fixed list of reader files imports from `lib/surprise`; the list is the
   one above, and a new reader not on it fails the test by name.
D. Budget: `buildBudget` takes the hidden set; hidden Costs are kept in
   `grandTotal`, `byStop`, `byDay`, `byChapter`, folded into a synthetic
   `byCategory` row with key `SURPRISE` and label "Surprise", and dropped
   from the line list. Cost vs paid both fold.

## Part 3 · Write guards

A. `requireStopAccess` / `requireItemAccess` and the per-entity checks in
   accommodation, attachments, costs, notes, reminders, day-titles and items
   refuse (`notFound()` semantics, as for a non-member) when the acting
   Traveller is surprised by the entity's live Surprise. Covered by
   `expectAccessCheckedBeforeWrite`.
B. `server/actions/surprise.ts`:
   - `setSurprise({ kind: "STOP"|"ITEM", id, forIds? })` — any Traveller who
     is not surprised by an enclosing Surprise; `forIds` defaults to every
     other member; the caller becomes planner. Re-calling by the planner
     changes the set. Records Activity "made Paris a surprise for Sam"
     (stamped as inside the Surprise, so the surprised do not see it; the
     shell's badge is what tells them).
   - `revealSurprise({ kind, id })` — planner only. Sets `revealedAt`,
     records "revealed the surprise in Paris" (unstamped).
   - `revealDueSurprises(now)` — stamps every live Surprise past its
     auto-reveal day and records the line as the planner, source "SYSTEM"
     wording "The surprise in Paris was revealed". Called from the digest
     cron route before dispatch. The lazy predicate in Part 2 already hides
     nothing by then, so the cron only makes the record.
C. `deleteStop` / `deleteItem` on a live Surprise set `revealedAt` first
   (the row cascades for a Stop; the Activity rows stay and un-hide because
   the Surprise is gone — treat a missing Surprise row as revealed).
D. `removeTripMember` / `leaveTrip`: reveal every live Surprise planned by
   the leaver; remove the leaver from every `surpriseForIds`.
E. `addMember` / accept Invite: no change (a joiner is not surprised).
F. Fork / promote / Duplicate copy the three columns (`fork-plan.ts`,
   `forks.ts` promote retag keeps them by construction, `duplicate-trip.ts`).

## Part 4 · UI

A. Stop card (desktop `stay-panel`/plan body and mobile `stop-sheet`):
   - Planner/helper view: badge "Surprise for Sam" (names joined with "and").
     Actions menu gains "Make it a surprise" (when no Surprise) or "Reveal
     the surprise" (planner only, when live). Two-member Trip: a confirm
     sheet; more: a picker with every other member ticked.
   - Surprised view: header and legs as today; body replaced by one line
     "Surprise, planned by Cam" with a gift icon; no actions menu, no add
     affordances, no drag handle.
B. Item: `item-form-dialog` gains "Surprise for…" beside Hidden from shares
   (same picker rules); `item-card` and `timeline` badge it; the surprised
   see a placeholder card at the date/time reading "Surprise, planned by
   Cam". Day section, Day view, Summary, Print, Calendar pages render the
   same shells.
C. Home: `next-steps-loader` adds, for the planner on the arrive date / the
   Item's day, "Reveal the surprise in Paris?" with a Reveal button.
   Flags and Next steps about the inside are not shown to the surprised.
D. Activity feed and bell: filtered rows; the reveal line reads naturally.

## Part 5 · Claude connection

A. Reads carry the shell and a `surprise` field (`{ plannedBy, forNames,
   youAreSurprised: boolean }`) on each Stop/Item that has one.
B. `MCP_INSTRUCTIONS` gains a paragraph: a Stop or Item marked as a surprise
   for you is being arranged for you; do not suggest, research, or ask what
   is inside, and do not try to add to it. (Also enforced by Part 3A.)
C. Tools `set_surprise` and `reveal_surprise` wrapping Part 3B, documented
   in `docs/connect-claude.md` under good things to ask.
