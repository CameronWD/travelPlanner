# Usage

Branch `feat/usage`, cut from `main` at `9a11ff71`. Agreed with Cam on 20 Nov 2026. The trip
leaves on 4 Dec and nothing deploys for five weeks after, so this must be live on main by
Fri 27 Nov or the data it exists to collect never exists.

**Why.** TEEPEE records every change to a Trip (Activity) and nothing about reading it.
After the trip Cam wants to know which screens and features each Traveller actually opened
while travelling, how fast they felt, whether saved copies carried the app with no signal,
and what the trip cost to run, so he can decide what earned its place.

## 1. What a Usage record is

One row for one of two things a signed-in Traveller did:

**A screen opened.** Recorded every time a Traveller lands on one of: Trips, Globe, Account,
Help, Admin, New trip, and inside a Trip: home, Plan, Day, Money, Calendar, Checklists,
Files, Activity, Summary, Print, Reviews, Wishlist, Journal, Settings, the Trip's Help. It
carries:
- the screen (a fixed name from that list, never a URL);
- the Trip, when the screen is inside one, and the Traveller;
- the Trip's phase at that moment (sketching, planning, travelling, past);
- how it was opened: installed app on a phone, phone browser, or desktop;
- how long it took to appear, in milliseconds, when the browser can say;
- whether it was answered from a saved copy with no connection;
- on the Day view only, the day's offset from today in whole days (0 today, 1 tomorrow,
  -2 two days ago), never the date.

**A named action**, from this fixed list and no other:
1. changed day on the Day view, and how: swipe, arrow, strip chip, keyboard;
2. opened the Day map;
3. tapped Directions on a thing to do;
4. opened a ticket or other Attachment;
5. opened the weather card's detail;
6. tapped "Got it" on a Reminder;
7. opened search;
8. opened the More sheet, and which row was chosen from it;
9. opened "Add to this day";
10. started a Booking import or a Review (Standard or Deep);
11. opened a shared Trip link while signed in.

Edits are not Usage: Activity already records them.

## 2. What is never recorded

- Anything a Traveller typed; any name, title, note, place, amount or file name; any URL or
  query string; any id other than the Trip's and the Traveller's.
- Time spent on a screen, scrolling, or any tap not on the list above.
- Anything on a public page (landing, Terms, Privacy, a shared Trip viewed signed out).
- **Surprise.** For a Traveller who has any Surprise hidden from them on that Trip, a Day
  view record carries no day offset and a "changed day" action carries only how. Which days
  someone opens could otherwise give a Surprise away to an Admin reading Usage, or give the
  planner's interest away to the surprised.

## 3. How it is recorded

- From the browser, after the page has drawn. Nothing a Traveller is waiting for waits on
  it, and a failure is silent.
- In batches: records collect in the browser and are sent together about every 30 seconds
  and when the app is hidden or closed.
- With no connection, records wait on the device and go up when it returns. The wait list
  is capped at 500 records; past that the oldest are dropped.
- The server takes the Traveller from the session, never from the request, checks each
  record against the fixed lists and drops anything it does not recognise, and refuses a
  Trip the Traveller is not on.
- No server page records anything while it is being built.
- Every signed-in Traveller, every Trip. No per-person setting.

## 4. Switch and keeping

- An Admin can turn recording off and on from the Admin screen. It is stored in the
  database, takes effect within a minute without a deploy, and is on by default. Off means
  browsers stop sending and the server stops storing.
- Records older than 180 days are deleted by the existing nightly housekeeping.
- A Usage record outlives its Trip and its Traveller's account only as a row with ids that
  point at nothing; deleting a Trip does not have to hunt them down.

## 5. Reading it

- **Admin screen, a Usage section**, Admins only. It shows whether recording is on, the
  total number of records and when the last one arrived. Pick a Trip: per Traveller and per
  day, the screens opened (counts), the named actions (counts), the typical time to appear
  (median) and how many opens came from a saved copy; and that Trip's AI and Google Maps
  cost from the records TEEPEE already keeps. A day is a calendar day in the Admin's own
  timezone. Counts only: it never lists single records.
- **A report script**, `npm run usage:report -- <trip>`, read-only against production like
  `feedback:pull`, printing the same figures as text so Claude can summarise them.

## 6. Around it

- `/privacy` gains a paragraph: what Usage records, that it is tied to the account, that
  Admins can read it, that it is kept 180 days, and what it never holds.
- `CONTEXT.md` gains **Usage**. An ADR records why this is first-party and not a tracker.
- Before it ships, time to appear and layout shift on the Trips list, trip home, Plan and
  the Day view are measured with recording on and off; they must not differ beyond noise.
- One migration (one table, one setting). Main is deployed and confirmed before beta.

## Not in this

Time on screen. Funnels, sessions or heatmaps. A per-Traveller opt-out. Usage for signed-out
visitors. Exporting Usage. Showing a Traveller their own Usage.
